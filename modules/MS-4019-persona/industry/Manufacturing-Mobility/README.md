# Manufacturing-Mobility · Plant planning coordinator

Help a planning coordinator turn fictional shift and supplier inputs into a coordination brief.

## Persona and task

A coordinator tracks supplier dependencies and reported scheduling blockers across teams.

## Starter prompt journey

1. Extract reported dependencies from synthetic supplier and shift updates.
2. Create an action table with evidence, owners, and open questions.
3. Draft a planning meeting agenda for human decision-makers.

## Success and review

Clearly separate assumptions from facts. Do not produce equipment operating instructions or replace safety procedures.
