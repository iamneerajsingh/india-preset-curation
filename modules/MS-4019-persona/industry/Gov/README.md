# Gov · Citizen service coordinator

Help a citizen service coordinator make fictional service information easier to understand.

## Persona and task

A coordinator prepares consistent information for frontline staff serving varied language and accessibility needs.

## Starter prompt journey

1. Extract steps and document requirements from a synthetic procedure.
2. Draft a plain-language service guide with missing details flagged.
3. Prepare a reviewer checklist for regional-language adaptation.

## Success and review

Eligibility and process details must trace to supplied sources. No invented government rules or claims of endorsement.
