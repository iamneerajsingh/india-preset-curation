# Telco · Customer service lead

Help a customer service lead turn fictional incident updates into clear audience-specific communication.

## Persona and task

A lead coordinates frontline messaging when service information is incomplete or changing.

## Starter prompt journey

1. Extract confirmed impact and unknowns from a synthetic incident log.
2. Draft an internal briefing with escalation points.
3. Create a customer-facing update without invented restoration estimates.

## Success and review

Use approved wording, reviewed language, and explicit unknowns. Exclude subscriber data and real network information.
