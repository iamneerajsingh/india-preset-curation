# Manufacturing-Mobility

Curate planning and coordination scenarios for manufacturing and mobility teams.

## Starter use case: shift and supplier coordination

Combine fictional shift notes and supplier updates into a planning summary.

## Prompt direction

List reported blockers, affected milestones, owners, and unanswered questions. Separate confirmed source facts from assumptions.

## India lens

Review regional supplier context and shift terminology. Do not generate equipment operating instructions or substitute for safety procedures.

## Expected output and readiness

A coordination brief for human review. **Draft:** source pack, safety boundaries, and owner approval pending.
